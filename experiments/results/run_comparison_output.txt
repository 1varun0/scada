Physical Insight on Detection Ratio (0.16 to 0.90 across slopes and seeds):
  Physics detection fires when total residual |h_obs - h_twin| = |bias(t) + noise(t) + e_twin(t)| > tau_p (3.5 cm).
  Across slopes 0.01 to 0.10 mm/s and 10 seeds, the individual ratio (bias/tau_p) spans 0.16 to 0.90.
  Slow-ramp detections are helped by sensor noise peaks (3*sigma ~ 1.5 cm) and twin integration error
  constructively adding to the residual, causing threshold crossing well before deterministic bias reaches tau_p.
  A constant bias below tau_p is never detected and shifts the pump cycling band by at most 3.5 cm.
