Core Physical Invariant:
  The Digital Twin integrates MEASURED flows: dh/dt = (q_in_obs - q_out_obs) / A.
  Therefore, plant pump-flow capacity (Q_max) and valve flow coefficient (Cv) errors CANNOT affect
  the twin's estimation, because any change in physical flow is measured by the flowmeters.
  Only two mechanisms cause twin estimation divergence:
    1. Tank cross-sectional area errors (A_plant != A_twin)
    2. Flow-sensor errors (measured q_in / q_out bias, or unmeasured physical leaks)
